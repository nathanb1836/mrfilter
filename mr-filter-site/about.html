Why we started

Filters are easy to forget. They're behind a furnace door, under a sink, or tucked in the back of the fridge. Most people only remember them when the air gets dusty, the water tastes off, or the fridge light starts blinking.

Changing them isn't hard. Keeping track of sizes, dates and which filter goes where is the part nobody wants to do. So we do it: we find every filter you have, set the right schedule, bring the parts, and keep a dated record of each change.

We're not a repair company and we don't try to sell you new equipment. If something looks wrong, we'll tell you and point you to a licensed pro.
