Air and water filters, changed on time.

Here's what we do for each kind of filter, in plain English.

Air filters Whole-house water Under-sink, fridge and RO

Air filters

Your furnace or HVAC system pulls air through a filter that catches dust, pet hair and pollen. When it fills up, your system works harder and your air gets dustier. We bring the right size, swap it, and write the date on the new one.

Visit time
About 15 minutes
How often
Usually every 1–3 months

Pets, allergies and thicker filters change the schedule. We'll set yours after the survey.

Whole-house water filters

This is the filter housing where water comes into your home. It catches sediment and grit before it reaches your taps and appliances. We shut off the water, swap the cartridge, check the seal, and turn it back on.

Visit time
About 20–30 minutes
How often
Usually every 3–6 months

Well water or heavy sediment may need more frequent changes. Your water stays off only for a few minutes.

Under-sink, fridge and RO filters

These filters clean the water you drink and cook with. We replace the cartridge in your fridge, the filter under your sink, and each stage of a reverse osmosis (RO) system, then flush the line so it's ready to use.

FilterHow often, usually
Fridge filterEvery 6 months
Under-sink filterEvery 6–12 months
RO pre- and post-filtersEvery 6–12 months
RO membraneEvery 2–3 years

Visit time: about 15 minutes for a fridge or single filter, up to 45 minutes for a full RO system.

If we find a leak or a failing system, we'll tell you and point you to a licensed pro. We don't do repairs. You'll see what we found in your photo report.
