What filters do you handle?+

Furnace and HVAC air filters, whole-house water filters, and drinking-water filters: under-sink, fridge, and every stage of a reverse osmosis (RO) system. Not sure what you have? That's what the free survey is for.

How do pricing and terms work?+

The Home plan is $199 a year on a 1-year term, $189 a year on 2 years, and $183 a year on 3 years. It covers up to 2 heating / AC units and 1 water filter cartridge, with filters included. Pay all upfront on a 2 or 3-year plan and the price drops further: $340 for 2 years or $483 for 3 years. Your price is locked for the whole term, and we confirm exactly what you have at your first visit.

What happens if I move?+

Let us know your new address. If it's in our service area, we'll survey the new home and move your plan over. If it isn't, we'll talk through your options. [Final moving policy to be confirmed.]

How do I pause or cancel?+

Call or email us and we'll take care of it. If you're travelling, we can skip a visit. Cancellation terms depend on your plan term and are spelled out before you sign up. [Pause and cancellation policy details to be added.]

What if I'm not home?+

You'll get a reminder by email or text before every visit. Reply to pick another time, or leave us access instructions in advance — many customers do. Business sites can name an on-site contact.

How are parts handled?+

Replacement filters are included in your plan. We bring the right ones based on your equipment record, bag up the old ones and take them away, and write the install date on each new filter.

Do you service my brand?+

Most likely. We work with the common sizes and brands of air filters, water filter housings, fridges and RO systems. During the survey we'll confirm we can source your filters. If we can't, we'll tell you before you sign up.

How does billing work?+

We keep a card or bank account on file and charge it automatically. Pay monthly on a 1-year plan, yearly, or all upfront on a 2 or 3-year plan for our best price. You'll get a receipt each time. Business accounts get one invoice covering every location.

Do you fix leaks or broken systems?+

No. If we find a leak or a failing system, we'll tell you, show it in your photo report, and point you to a licensed pro. We don't do repairs.
