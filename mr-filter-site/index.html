Questions?

What filters do you handle?+

Furnace and HVAC air filters and whole-house water filters are in the Home Plan. Under-sink, fridge and reverse osmosis (RO) filters can be added.

Are the filters really included?+

Yes. Your plan price covers the visit and the filters for up to 2 air units and 1 water cartridge. No separate filter bill.

What if I'm not home?+

We remind you before each visit. Reply to pick another time, or leave us access instructions.

How does billing work?+

You pay online when you sign up. We keep your card or bank account on file and charge it automatically, yearly or monthly.
